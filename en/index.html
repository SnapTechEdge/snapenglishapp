会話内容

相手Hi! What can I get for you?

あなたI'd like a hot latte, please.

相手Sure. What size would you like?

あなたI'll have a medium, please.

相手For here or to go?

あなたFor here, please.

相手That'll be four fifty. How would you like to pay?

あなたCan I pay by card?

相手Thank you! It'll be ready shortly.

あなたThanks. I'll wait.

覚えておきたい表現

I'd like a hot latte, please.注文を丁寧に伝える定番フレーズ。"I want" より柔らかく自然。
I'll have a medium, please.サイズや品物を選ぶときに使える口語的な表現。
For here, please."For here or to go?" に対して店内飲食を伝える一言。
Can I pay by card?カード払いを希望するときの自然な確認フレーズ。

あなたの回答

I did overwork yesterday night.

回答例

1.0x 0.8x

I worked overtime last night.

改善点

1.単語の選択

did overwork

worked overtime

overwork は「過度に働く」という意味で、「残業をする」という意味の overtime とは異なります。

2.表現の自然さ

yesterday night

last night

「昨夜」は last night が自然な表現で、yesterday night は不自然です。

別の言い方

I had to work overtime last night.

had to を付けると「残業せざるを得なかった」という含みが出ます。事実だけを述べるなら worked overtime です。

Spaced repetition (SRS)

Make what you got right
harder to forget.

Questions you have answered come back at widening intervals — the next day first, then further apart or closer together depending on how you do.

  • The gap before a question returns adapts to how you did (1 day → 3 → 7 → 21 → 60)
  • Separate review for composition, grammar and vocabulary
  • A “today’s review” queue keeps it a daily habit
  • Bookmark any question to add it to your review list

FAQ

What people ask
before starting.

What is SnapEnglish?
SnapEnglish is an English learning app developed and published by SnapTechEdge. It covers AI conversation, instant Japanese-to-English composition, grammar and vocabulary in a single app. You study by typing, speaking and arranging words. See About SnapEnglish for more.
Do I need to sign in to use the app?
You can use the basic features without signing in. Sign in with your Apple ID or Google account only if you want to sync your progress across multiple devices.
Will I be charged automatically when the trial ends?
No. Nothing is charged during the 7-day trial. After the trial, you choose whether to subscribe, and only if you want to keep using the premium features. Once subscribed, you can cancel at any time from your store settings — Subscriptions in your Apple ID account settings, or Subscriptions in Google Play.
Can I keep using the app for free after the trial?
No. A subscription is required to keep using the app after the trial. When the trial ends you will see the plans and a “Restore purchases” screen. If you already subscribed — for example after changing devices — restore it from that screen.
What are the system requirements?
iPhone (iOS 18.0 or later), iPad (iPadOS 18.0 or later) and Android (8.0 or later).
Is it available for Android?
Yes. The Android version is available on Google Play.
Do voice features work offline?
Please have an internet connection for voice input when answering. On iOS it uses Apple’s speech recognition service, which requires a connection. On Android it uses the speech recognition built into your device, so it may work offline depending on the device and its settings. AI feedback and pronunciation analysis always require an internet connection.
Can I connect it to Studyplus?
SnapEnglish is registered as a Studyplus partner app. Once connected, the time you study in SnapEnglish is recorded on your Studyplus timeline — usually as a single daily entry, posted automatically from the next day.

How to connect
1. Install the Studyplus app on your device and sign in
2. In SnapEnglish, open 設定 (Settings) and tap Studyplus 連携 (Studyplus integration)
3. Tap Studyplus と連携する (Connect to Studyplus) and approve it on the screen that appears

To stop, tap 連携を解除 (Disconnect) on the same screen.

Studyplus is a study-logging service for learners in Japan, and its app is in Japanese.
How is my personal data handled?
We collect your account details (email address and user ID), your learning data and your purchase history. In addition, only when you use the AI features, the English you type or speak and your pronunciation-practice audio are sent to AI services (we ask for your consent the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings). When you use voice input, your audio may also be sent to a speech recognition service for transcription. We do not do any advertising tracking. For details, see our Privacy Policy.
