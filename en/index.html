HOW IT WORKS

Stop just memorizing English.
Say it or write it, fix it, use it.

You produce the English yourself instead of taking it in passively. That's why it comes out when the moment arrives.

Vocabulary stocks the raw material, grammar drills the word order, composition is where you write it, conversation is where you say it and use it. All four sit on this one loop.

  1. STEP 01

    Say it or write it

    Build the English yourself from the meaning — by voice or by typing. Either way, the words are yours.

  3. STEP 02

    Fix

    AI corrects it with reasons and shows the natural phrasing — so you learn why it's said that way.

  5. STEP 03

    Use

    Use the corrected English again — this time on your own. It's only yours once you've used it.

What you notice you're missing feeds your next attempt. The more you loop, the more you speak.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign in to use the app?

You can use the basic features without signing in. Sign in with Apple ID or Google only if you want to sync your progress across multiple devices.

Will I be charged automatically when the trial ends?

No. The app does not require a subscription during the trial. After 7 days, you only subscribe if you want to keep using premium features. You can cancel anytime from your Apple ID subscription settings.

What are the system requirements?

iPhone (iOS 18.0+) and iPad (iPadOS 18.0+).

Is it available for Android?

Currently in development. We will announce the release date here once it is confirmed.

How is my personal data handled?

We collect your account details (email address and user ID), learning data, and purchase history. In addition, only when you use the AI features, the English you type or speak and your pronunciation-practice audio are sent to AI services — we ask for your consent the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings. We do no advertising tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do voice features work offline?

Voice input (when answering) is processed on-device, so it works offline. AI feedback and pronunciation analysis require an internet connection.
