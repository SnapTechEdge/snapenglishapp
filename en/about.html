About

What is SnapEnglish?

SnapEnglish is an English learning app developed and published by SnapTechEdge. It brings AI conversation practice, instant composition, grammar, vocabulary and spaced repetition together in a single app.

What you can do

  • AI conversation — Complete a real-world task in English with an AI partner, such as checking rental car conditions. You can follow a script or speak freely in your own words.
  • Instant composition — Turn a Japanese sentence into English on the spot and get AI feedback on clarity, grammar and naturalness.
  • Grammar — Build sentences by arranging words to internalise sentence patterns and word order.
  • Vocabulary — Study leveled vocabulary with example sentences.
  • Spaced repetition (SRS) — Questions come back just before you forget them, so they stick.

Developer

SnapEnglish is developed and published by SnapTechEdge, which is also the seller listed on the App Store and Google Play. For the legal seller information, see 特定商取引法に基づく表記 (Japanese only).

Requirements

Requires iPhone (iOS 18.0+), iPad (iPadOS 18.0+), or Android (8.0+). The interface and all explanations are in Japanese; the language you study is English.

How you study

You learn by typing, speaking and arranging words. The app does not include any photo or image recognition based learning.

Download

Available on the App Store and Google Play, free to try.
